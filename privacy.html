Privacy, briefly.

This covers the two SWING websites: swingsofia.com and menu.swingsofia.com. They are run by SWING (Swingin' Hall), Sofia, Bulgaria. Phone: 089 684 0161.

Analytics and ads

Our sites use two tools from outside companies:

  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) — records a visit, the page (for example the menu) and whether a phone number was tapped, and sets a cookie (_fbp) so Meta can recognise the same browser later. We use it to measure our Instagram and Facebook posts and to show our gigs to people who have visited. Meta's policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy; you can limit ads based on your activity under "activity off Meta technologies" in your Facebook or Instagram settings.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd.) — anonymous statistics: how many visits, from where, on what device, which sections get read. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

On swingsofia.com a cookie bar lets you accept or decline these tools. Your answer is kept in one cookie (swing_consent) for 180 days.

Hosting and fonts

  • The sites are hosted by Vercel Inc., which keeps standard server logs (IP address, time, page) for security.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts; your browser sends your IP address to Google to fetch them.
  • The language switch and nothing else is remembered in your browser's local storage.

Change your mind

Tap the button to forget your cookie choice; the bar on swingsofia.com will ask again on your next visit. You can also clear cookies for swingsofia.com in your browser.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its use. In practice we hold nothing that identifies you by name; the tools above work with cookie identifiers. Write to us at the venue or call the number above. You can also complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (cpdp.bg).

Статистика и реклама

Сайтовете ни използват два инструмента на външни компании:

  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) — записва посещение, страницата (например менюто) и дали е натиснат телефонен номер, и поставя бисквитка (_fbp), за да може Meta да разпознае същия браузър по-късно. Използваме го, за да измерваме публикациите си в Instagram и Facebook и да показваме концертите си на хора, които са ни посещавали. Политика на Meta: facebook.com/privacy/policy; можеш да ограничиш рекламите според активността си от „активност извън технологиите на Meta“ в настройките на Facebook или Instagram.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd.) — анонимна статистика: брой посещения, откъде, от какво устройство, кои секции се четат. Политика на Google: policies.google.com/privacy.

На swingsofia.com лента за бисквитки ти позволява да приемеш или откажеш тези инструменти. Отговорът ти се пази в една бисквитка (swing_consent) за 180 дни.
