    Est. 1995 · Sofia

    Still here, folks.

    Swingin' Hall opened in 1995 and has been loud ever since - the oldest standing jazz & rock stage in town. These days it goes by SWING: a new, modern look, the same punk soul.

    Jazz, rock, grunge, the occasional acoustic one-man show. Name a famous Bulgarian band or musician - chances are their path started on this stage.

    Come for the gig. Stay for the bar. No apologies.
